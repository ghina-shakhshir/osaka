What is Homco interior design service?

Homco is a home interior design studio that helps you create modern and minimalist home interiors with a complete concept, layout, and decoration.

How does the design process work?

We start from consultation, then planning, 3D concept, and execution. Each step is discussed with the client before moving forward to the next stage.

Can you work with small apartment spaces?

Yes, we have a lot of experience designing small apartments and maximizing limited spaces with smart layouts and furniture choices.

Do you provide 3D design before execution?

Yes, we provide 3D and 2D design previews so you can see how your home interior will look before we start the real work.

How long does a typical project take?

The duration depends on the size and complexity of the project. On average, home interior design projects take 4–8 weeks from concept to finishing.

Can you help with furniture selection only?

Yes, we also offer partial services such as furniture selection, decoration, and layout improvements without full renovation.
